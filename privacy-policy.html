Cookies and Tracking

Our sites use cookies for personalization and analytics. In general, a cookie is a small file containing a string of characters that is sent to your browser when you visit a mobile application or website. When you visit again, the cookie allows the mobile application or website to recognize your browser or device. Cookies also may store user preferences and other information. Cookies offer a form of convenience in that they save the user time by allowing the mobile application or website to identify preferences that the user has displayed or selected in the past.

We and our service providers may use cookies, session replay, and similar technologies to analyze how users interact with our services, including pages or screens viewed, unredacted screen capture images of your session while using or interacting with certain components of our app or platform, features used, clicks, scrolling activity, and navigation paths, session activity, and related usage interactions. Where appropriate, we mask sensitive information. We may also use these technologies to document and improve the claim submission process.

Social media plugins may transmit your IP address and profile information to social networks, even if you are not logged in to those services.

You can generally decide whether to accept, block, or reject cookies through your own browser or mobile device, but we are not responsible for making this selection for you. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our services, but your access to some functionality and components may be restricted. For more information on how cookies work and how to control them, you may visit www.allaboutcookies.org.
